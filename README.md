# Forward Deployed Engineer — Interview Questions

A curated collection of interview questions (with answers) for **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** roles — a distinctive software engineering role, popularized by Palantir and now adopted across a growing number of AI, data, and enterprise software companies, defined less by a specific technical domain and more by *how* the work happens: embedded directly with customers, building and rapidly adapting real software against messy, high-stakes, real-world operational problems, often under significant ambiguity about what the actual requirements even are.

This is a real, increasingly common title, but it's also a genuinely distinctive discipline within software engineering — it combines core software engineering skill (data integration, application development, systems thinking) with a customer-facing, product-discovery orientation more commonly associated with product management or consulting, and a specific tolerance for ambiguity, rapid iteration, and direct ownership of outcomes that differs meaningfully from a typical product engineering role building against a well-specified roadmap. The questions here reflect that combination — from "how do you design an integration against an enterprise system with no clean API" to "how do you tell a customer their requested feature is the wrong solution to their actual problem."

> **Disclaimer:** This is a community knowledge base, not a certification or official curriculum. Answers aim to be technically sound and interview-appropriate — concise, structured, demonstrating reasoning — not exhaustive textbook chapters. Company-specific practices vary considerably in this role more than in most; treat company-specific process descriptions as illustrative rather than universal.

## 🧭 How to use this repo

- **Candidates:** Work through categories in order — the role fundamentals (1) and requirements discovery (2) establish the mindset everything else builds on, since FDE work is as much about figuring out the right problem as building the right solution. Category 10 (system design/case studies) and category 11 (behavioral) are where this role is most commonly and most rigorously evaluated in real interview loops, since raw technical depth alone doesn't capture what makes someone effective in this specific role.
- **Interviewers:** Pick 1–2 questions per category rather than exhausting every category in one loop. This role rewards candidates who can reason concretely about tradeoffs under ambiguity and incomplete information — watch for candidates who default to a single "right answer" regardless of context versus those who ask clarifying questions and reason explicitly about tradeoffs.
- **Everyone:** Answers are written to show *reasoning* — why a particular integration approach fits a specific constraint, why moving fast on a prototype doesn't mean skipping architectural judgment — not just to state facts.
